Brilliant Dyes Recognition at Imperial Graduation 2026

Brilliant Dyes was proudly recognised by Imperial College London President Professor Hugh R. Brady at the 2026 Imperial Graduation Ceremony, a landmark moment for our team and a testament to our deep roots within this remarkable institution.

Imperial's Department of Chemical Engineering is the very place where the idea of harnessing microalgae to transform the textile dyeing industry first took root. To be recognised on stage, in front of Imperial's graduating class of 2026, is a brilliant and deeply personal milestone.

"Scaling fast, raising significant investment, and earning global recognition for technologies that transform material design and decarbonise the textile industry."

— Professor Hugh R. Brady, President of Imperial College London

We are proud to call Imperial our home, and this recognition inspires us to keep pushing forward.
