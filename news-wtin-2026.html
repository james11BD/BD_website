Brilliant Dyes Featured in WTiN Innovation Outlook H2 2026

Brilliant Dyes has been featured as the lead entry in the Dyes & Inks section of the WTiN Innovation Outlook H2 2026 — a Special Report on Dyes, Inks & Finishes published by the World Textile Information Network. The report identifies the most commercially significant emerging innovations across the global textile value chain, and Brilliant Dyes' algae-derived natural dye platform is highlighted as a technology to watch.

WTiN classifies Brilliant Dyes at the Emerging stage with a Technology Readiness Level of 5, signalling a technology that has moved beyond proof-of-concept and is ready for scale-up. The company is targeting a pre-seed funding round in Q4 2026, with pilot production capacity of 5–10 tonnes per year expected by Q3 2027.

"Naturally abundant algae utilisation gives us the edge of producing natural bioactive colour materials, which are already in the existing safe materials list for even cosmetics and foods. It gives us the opportunity to scale globally, even in open systems, without the need for class 2 or 3 biosafety labs or closed, confined, expensive reactors, which are necessary for genetically modified organisms (GMOs)."

— Redwan Rahman, Co-founder of Brilliant Dyes

The WTiN report underscores the environmental credentials that set Brilliant Dyes apart from conventional synthetic dye production. The platform offers substantial reductions across every major environmental metric:

The WTiN Innovation Outlook is an authoritative benchmarking resource used by industry executives and R&D teams across the global textile supply chain. Being selected as the lead innovation in the Dyes & Inks special report reflects growing industry recognition of Brilliant Dyes as a credible, near-commercial solution to one of fashion's most entrenched sustainability challenges.
