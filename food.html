Our
food

Natural colour for food

gummy bears and gums

Our natural pigments give food and beverage producers a way to replace artificial colourings, facing increasing regulatory and consumer pressure, with a clean-label alternative derived entirely from algae. As the algae we use are already recognised as safe for food applications, our colours can be implemented across confectionery, coatings, drinks and more. Our first food product, a batch of naturally coloured gummy bears, shows how our shades hold up in a real formulation, delivering bright, appealing colour without artificial additives. It's an early but tangible step toward helping the industry move away from synthetic dyes while keeping the vivid colours consumers expect.
