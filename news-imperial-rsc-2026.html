Imperial College London Celebrates Brilliant Dyes' RSC Award Win

Brilliant Dyes has been awarded the Royal Society of Chemistry's Change Makers Spotlight Award at the RSC Emerging Technologies Competition 2026. The Change Makers Spotlight Award is the highest recognition in the competition, given to the single standout innovation across all categories judged to have exceptional potential to address major global challenges through chemistry-led innovation.

"This award is a powerful endorsement of our vision to harness chemistry and engineering biology to solve some of the world's most pressing environmental challenges. We believe the future of chemistry should not only perform better, it should leave the world better."

— Redwan Rahman & Dr Tabish Noori, Co-founders of Brilliant Dyes

The RSC Emerging Technologies Competition showcases early-stage technologies with the potential for real-world impact across health, energy, enabling technologies, and environmental sectors. Finalists receive mentoring and training before presenting to panels of industry experts, investors, and entrepreneurs — making the Spotlight Award a recognition not only of scientific innovation but of commercial and societal potential.

"It is fantastic to see Brilliant Dyes recognised with the Change Makers Spotlight Award. This achievement reflects the creativity, determination and collaborative spirit that define innovation in the Department of Chemical Engineering."

— Professor Omar Matar, Head of Department, Chemical Engineering, Imperial College London

Brilliant Dyes is one of the success stories of ChemEng Enterprise, a strategic translation programme launched in 2019 by Imperial's Department of Chemical Engineering. Unique within Imperial's departments, ChemEng Enterprise supports researchers and students in developing entrepreneurship skills, forming businesses, and accessing pre-launch financing and team building — having helped launch one in six of Imperial's deep-tech spinouts over six years.

We would also like to congratulate the other award winners from the competition: Alphavectors Biotech, runner-up in the Health category, and e-ReBat, runner-up in the Energy category. It was an honour to compete alongside such impressive innovations.
