Brilliant Dyes Named Finalist in the Royal Society of Chemistry Emerging Technologies Competition

We are thrilled to announce that Brilliant Dyes has been named a finalist in the Royal Society of Chemistry Emerging Technologies Competition 2026 — one of the UK's most prestigious awards recognising innovative companies developing transformative science with real-world impact.

The RSC Emerging Technologies Competition celebrates breakthrough technologies at the forefront of chemistry and materials science. Being named a finalist is a powerful validation of our mission to make the textile industry cleaner, greener, and more accountable through microalgae-derived natural dyes.

We are proud to be recognised alongside some of the UK's most exciting emerging technology companies, and we look forward to representing Brilliant Dyes at the next stage of the competition.
